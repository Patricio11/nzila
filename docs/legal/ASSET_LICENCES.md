## What the Open Font License asks of us

- The font may be used, embedded and shipped in the game, including when the game earns money.
- The font file may not be sold by itself.
- The copyright notice and the licence travel with the font: they are in this directory, which ships in the repository.
- The licence declares no Reserved Font Name for Fraunces, so the cut-down file keeps the name.
- Anyone else may use Fraunces too. What is ours alone is what we draw (D-0.38).
