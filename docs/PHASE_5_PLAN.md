# Phase 5 plan: Casa

*Goal (roadmap): a place of your own, that looks like you, where friends can come.*

Opened 2026-11-20, when Phase 4 completed (`docs/completed/PHASE_4_COMPLETE.md`). **It is built after** the things to do together (6.2's first line, 10.13's first two), **the second bairro, Zango** (11.1, 11.2) and the alpha (4B): the founder's order after Phase 4 (D-4.8 to D-4.10). Zango comes first for this phase too: **the family gives the house in the family's bairro** (D-3.13), and most families' bairros are not the Marginal.

## Open questions

- **Q1 The house each family gives**, by how well off it is: a room, a casa de chapa, a casa de bloco, a vivenda (D-3.13 asks for it in tunables, beside the families). *Proposed: aperto a room or a casa de chapa, remediado a casa de bloco, bem a bigger one, ouro a vivenda. The founder's.* **Built so far (D-5.8): a family of little money is given a house of Zango, the one the State delivered. The families that wake on the Marginal (remediado, bem, ouro) have no house yet: the Marginal has none built to give, and no new bairro is begun (D-5.6).** Which kind each of them is given, and where, is still the founder's.
- **Q2 What rent costs.** `docs/ECONOMY.md` §6: an hour of play earns about 2 400 Mk beyond the card and costs 500; money piles up until the sinks that matter exist, and rent is the first. *Proposed: a week's rent for a room about three hours of biscatos (some 7 000 Mk), a T1 twice that; in a rules file, with a grace period. The founder's.*
- **Q3 The inside of an ordinary house** (Waiting item 18: still wanted): photographs of a sala, a quarto, a cozinha and a quintal, so that the interiors are Angolan homes and not a catalogue's.
- **Q4 How much an interior may weigh**, for the first view of it on a slow network. *Proposed: 300 KB at Poupança, fetched at the door, kept once fetched.*
- **Q5 Who may come in.** A visit is asked at the door and let in by someone inside; a key is given by the owner, and taken back. *Recommendation: as the roadmap; the founder's to confirm whether a kamba may come in when nobody is home.*

## Decisions

**D-5.1 An interior is its own small place, entered through the door (delegated).** A home's inside is built from a layout template (quarto, T1, T2, vivenda) as a small pack of its own, entered without a loading screen when it is fetched (a short fade otherwise, UX_UI_SPEC.md §3.5); the street goes on outside its window. Who is inside is the zone's, as the street is. *Recommendation: yes.*

**D-5.2 The family's house cannot be lost (delegated, from D-3.13).** It costs nothing and is never taken for rent or debt; a rented home can be lost, and what was in it is stored, never deleted. *Recommendation: yes.*

**D-5.3 Rent is a weekly job of the worker, through the ledger (delegated).** Notices before it is due (Mensagens), a grace period, and an eviction that stores belongings. The `rent` sink. *Recommendation: yes.*

**D-5.4 Furniture is a thing like any other (delegated).** Bought, carried (or brought by a kupapata), held in the home's own place in `items`, and placed on a grid; what is placed is "à vista", what is stored is not (Phase 7's caps read it). *Recommendation: yes.*

**D-5.5 What a competitor's players ask of a home (founder, 2026-11-21; `docs/reference/MARKET_NOTES.md` §1g).** Four things this phase is held to. **A player wakes where they went to sleep**: indoors is kept by the zone as the street is, and nobody who went home wakes at a paragem. **Two who are in the same home see each other**: a home is a room of the zone, not a screen each has alone. **A party is heard**: its columns play (our own synthesised music, D-0.x, no licence and no megabytes). **Rent is never taken from someone who is not playing** beyond a grace they were told of (D-5.3 already gives notices). And furniture is chosen by room.

**D-5.6 Interiors first, and no new bairro until what a bairro is made of is built (the founder, 2026-11-22). The alpha opens when the family's house can be gone into: confirmed by the founder the same day ("yes that is what I meant").** *"Let's do interior first because we are going to need it in other bairros. Let's stop other bairros for now: we already have the Marginal, the Miradouro and Zango. To implement the next bairro like Cazenga we are going to use almost everything we would have created already."* This changes the order of D-4.8: Cazenga and the bairros after it wait; Phase 5 begins now, with being inside. **One way of being inside for every kind of place.** A home, a bar, a salão, a cantina, a classroom and a church are entered the same way and seen the same three ways (D-0.41: at eye level through the door, cut away from above, and seated through one's own eyes), from one piece of the game: an interior is a small place of its own made from a template (D-5.1), who is in it is the zone's, and two who are inside see each other (D-5.5). What differs between a quarto and a salão is the template and what may be done there, not the code. **The order inside Phase 5** (each a commit or a few, each seen on a phone before the next): (1) *the inside itself*: one template (a room of block: floor, walls, a door, a window the street's light comes through), its own small room in the zone, going in and coming out through a door that swings (5.1's "doors that are doors"), the three views and the button between them, the weight of it measured (Q4); entered first from the houses of Zango; (2) *the family's house* (D-3.13, Q1): each player's own, where they wake and where they went to sleep; (3) *things in it* (5.3): bought, carried home, put where the player wants; (4) *who comes in* (5.4); then renting and the rest of the phase. **Places that are not homes** take the same inside as soon as (1) stands: the bar, the salão and the barbearia, the cantina (their owning is Phase 8, their inside is not), and the church (its culto is Phase 9, its inside is not). **What the founder was told, 2026-11-22, and has not answered:** the alpha (4B.3 to 4B.5) was to open before Phases 5 to 10, so that their order came from what real players come back for (4B.5); building every phase first is months without one real player, and the recommendation is to open the alpha when the family's house can be gone into, not after Phase 10.

**D-5.7 A place that is gone into stands in the street where it stands (delegated; the founder: "all views, how you move and view the inside", 2026-11-22; built the same day).** D-5.1 said an interior is a small pack of its own, entered with a fade. Built otherwise, for a better reason than the plan had: **the building in the street has real walls, a real doorway and real window openings** (`kit_baixa.sala`: a floor, four walls 30 cm thick, a roof that is also the ceiling), and the player walks in at the door. Nothing is loaded and nothing fades; the street is seen through the windows from inside and the room from the street (I-14: the window is the feature); whoever is inside is seen by whoever is inside, because they are in the same street of the same channel, and the zone needed nothing new: the walls are in the walk grid, taken from what is built. The pack says which rectangles are insides (`insides`: the rectangle, the doorway, the height of the walls). **The three ways of seeing it (D-0.41)**, one button under the wallet, the choice kept for each place on the phone (`apps/client/src/dentro/`, fetched when the bairro has such places): *à altura dos olhos*, behind the shoulder, as far back as the room allows and never through a wall (so near a wall that the body would fill the picture, the body is not drawn); *de cima*, the camera over the room and the world cut off just under the top of the walls, so the roof is gone and the room is whole (nearer for a small room); *pelos teus olhos*, from any seat: the camera at the eyes, turned by the thumb, the player's own body out of the way, and standing it says to sit. **First places:** Zango's igreja (a hall of ten pews, the table and the cross, windows down both sides) and the Salão da Dona Nela (the mirror, two chairs under dryers, a bench under the window; the cut is now done inside). **What this leaves for a home (step 2):** a family's house is one of a hundred and fifty in a street and each belongs to many players, one in each of their own lives: the house stands where it stands for all of them, and who sees whom inside is the zone's to keep apart (a home is its family's; the street does not see into it). And for every place: **doors** that swing on their hinges and are solid when shut (5.1); until then a doorway stands open.

**D-5.8 The family's house: one of the bairro's own houses, shut to the street and open to its family (delegated; built 2026-11-22).** The founder: *"step 2, the family's house, with doors once there is room for them."* What was decided in building it, and why.

- **Which house.** A bairro's pack lists the houses it has to give (`homes`: where each stands, the way its door looks, its kind, the paint of its door), and the registry says how many (`content/registry.json`: Zango, 116 of the kind `realojamento`; the pack's build refuses a bairro that does not have that many). The API gives a player theirs the first time it is asked for (`GET /v1/me/casa`, and on coming into the street): in the bairro the card of origin wakes them in, **the house with the fewest families**, the dice choosing among equals (`family_homes`, migration 0018). Given once; never lost (D-5.2); in the player's data and gone with their account. A bairro has more families than houses, so **many families are given the same house, and each has its own inside**.
- **A wall to the street, a home to its family.** On the street's walk grid every house is solid, doorway and all. For its family alone, the zone lays the house's own ground over the street's (`packages/world/src/casas.ts`, the same rule on the phone, so that what the phone works out is what the zone decides): they walk in at the door, and nobody else can. No lock is needed to keep a stranger out, and none was built.
- **Who sees whom.** The zone knows who is inside which family's house (`Channel`: a place of one's own, and the key it is under). **Whoever is inside sees only who is inside with them; the street sees only the street.** Two families of one house stand in the same walls and never meet, and are not in each other's way. It is the same both ways on purpose: everything that reads "who sees whom" (talk, a wave, a handshake, a gift) stays true without a rule of its own. The price: from the window the street's people who are players are not seen (the street's own people and vehicles are). An idea for later, not a defect.
- **The inside is drawn once.** The house in the street is a shell with a real doorway (`kit.casa_de_realojamento`). Its inside (`art/scripts/kit_casas.py`: a cement floor, white walls, the door between two windows, one side window, an esteira to wake on) is one model of 232 triangles and 5 KB in a pack of its own (`pnpm pack:casas`, served at `/packs/casas/`), set by the phone in the player's own house and in no other. Hollowing all 116 houses in the pack would have cost every visitor some 25 000 triangles and megabytes for rooms they can never enter. From inside, the shell's outer faces are not drawn, so **the street is seen through the windows for real**; from outside a house's windows stay dark, and nobody looks in.
- **Doors that are doors, for homes.** No door is drawn in the pack. The phone hangs all 116 as one model (sixteen triangles a door, each in its house's paint, with a handle), shut. **The player's own door swings open on its hinge as they come to it, from either side, and shuts behind them.** It is solid when shut because the ground behind it is not theirs to walk unless the house is. What 5.1 asks that this is not yet: a hand on the handle, a door that stays as it was left, a lock that rattles, and other people's doors seen to open (someone going home is seen to walk up to a shut door and be gone).
- **Waking at home.** Someone who has never stood in their bairro wakes in their house, standing beside the esteira and turned to their door; someone who closed the game at home wakes where they stood (D-5.5: the zone keeps indoors as it keeps the street). Off a candongueiro, one still arrives at the paragem.
- **Finding it.** One house among many that are the same: a small gold sign turns over the roof of the player's own, and the Mapa shows it ("A tua casa", with its number), to be marked for the compass or ridden to.
- **Measured.** The first download: 299,3 KB of 300 with all of this in (it was 299,9 before: room was made, F-287). The inside: 5,1 KB, fetched once in a bairro that has houses. The pack of Zango: the same triangles as before (91 232 near), 0,55 MB at Poupança. The doors: 1 856 triangles in every picture of Zango.

**D-5.9 Everyone has a house where houses are built; a door is opened and shut by hand; the family worries when it is late (the founder, 2026-11-22).** His words: *"Everyone should just have a house in Zango. If they wake up on the Marginal they should take a taxi to go home and should have direction where their house is. When it gets late they must be warned that they can't sleep outside or they might get robbed. Because we only have houses in Zango we should allocate houses only in bairros that we have."* And of the door: *"they should open the door and close, you know, like real life. Algumas coisas você sabe, não preciso te falar."* And: *"this game, we are going to add other provinces and potentially countries like Portugal, Brazil, South Africa. We must build with that in mind: players might even be able to travel to another country to live or holiday."*

- **Houses are given only where houses are built.** A family whose card wakes it in a bairro with no houses is given one in a bairro that has: of the same province first, and failing that any (`homeOf` in `apps/api/src/app.ts`). Today that means every family's house is in Zango, and a family that wakes on the Marginal goes home by candongueiro. **Nothing names a bairro in the rule**: it reads the registry, and a province or a country added later gives its own houses by it. Where one lives (`family_homes`: a district's id) is kept apart from where one is; a second home in another country is another row of the same kind, when renting and travel are built.
- **Told where home is.** Arriving in a bairro that is not theirs, a player reads for a quarter of a minute "A tua casa é no Zango. Para ir para casa, apanha o candongueiro."; the family's message in Mensagens gives the house's number and says the Mapa shows which paragem the van leaves from; in the home's bairro the Mapa marks the house and a gold sign turns over its roof (D-5.8).
- **Late.** From eight in the evening to five in the morning, whoever has a house and is not in it reads, in the cloth's red: "Já é tarde. Vai para casa: quem dorme na rua pode ser roubado." (and where home is, when it is far). **It is a warning and nothing more yet**: sleeping, and being robbed asleep in the street, are 5.2 and 7.2, and the words are true of the game only when those are built. Said plainly in F-288.
- **A door as in life.** The player's own door is shut until a hand opens it: standing at it, on either side, a gold button says "Abrir a porta" or "Fechar a porta" (`MSG.PORTA`). **Shut, it is a wall to its own family too**; open, it stays open until somebody shuts it, whoever comes and goes. The zone keeps every door as it was left and tells everyone (`EVENT.PORTA`): **a neighbour's door is seen to swing**, and behind it is the dark of a house one cannot see into. It is not shut on anyone standing in the doorway. Families given the same house share its door (one house, one door); their insides stay their own. An open door does not let a stranger in: the house is still a wall to them, lock or no lock.
- **From inside, the street.** Whoever is in their house sees the players in the street through the window and the open door, and hears them; the street does not see in, and nothing reaches through a wall (a wave, a gift, a handshake: `apart` in the zone's room).
- **A sala and a quarto, and a bulb.** The inside has a wall between a sala (the front door, one window) and a quarto (the other window, the side window, the esteira), with a doorway between them. A bulb hangs in the sala: after dark the room is lit by it.
- **The button is not the one gold button.** The hand to the door is a button of its own, in the middle over the place where words are written: the gold button's choice is made in the way in, which has 0,6 KB left (F-287). One hand for everything is the right end; it waits for room there.

**D-5.10 One wakes in a public place of the bairro one lives in, until one has slept at home (the founder, 2026-11-22).** His words, asked whether a family of the Marginal should wake at home in Zango: *"Yes, they should wake at their home in Zango, or actually in a public place, you know, and they can go to their home. That way they can meet and see other players: they can wake by the geladaria or next to a restaurant. But if they go home, next time they should wake up where they slept."* What is built:

- **Everyone wakes in the bairro their house is in.** Before a player has stood anywhere, they are where their house is (`bairroOf` in the API), not where the card of origin says: a phone that opens the Marginal is taken to Zango. With no house built anywhere, the card's rule stands (D-11.1).
- **In a public place, among people.** A bairro's pack names its waking places (`spawns` of the kind `first_arrival`: Zango's are the middle of the bairro, before the geladaria, by the kiosk bar and on the largo of the cantina); the zone sets each newcomer down at one of them, the same one for the same player. Nobody's first sight of the game is an empty room. From there the Mapa shows the way home (D-5.8).
- **Whoever sleeps at home wakes at home.** The zone already keeps where a player stood when they closed the game: at home, that is where they wake, turned to their door. It is the same rule for the street: one wakes where one stopped. (Sleep as a thing one does, on a bed, is 5.2 and 5.3.)
- This reverses D-5.8's "someone who has never stood in their bairro wakes in their house", the same day, on the founder's word.

**D-5.11 Things in the house: bought, carried home, set down where one stands (Task 5.3's first piece; built 2026-11-22).** The founder, 2026-11-18: the things of a house are bought at the shopping or at the market, carried home, and put wherever in the house the player wants; a thing never sinks into a wall or floats, never vanishes on the way, and stays exactly where it was left. What was decided in building it.

- **Furniture is a thing like any other (D-5.4).** A kind of the catalogue, `mobilia` (`packages/economy/tunables/items.json`): a plastic chair, a plastic table, a mattress, a bed, a sofa, a television; each with the room it takes in the Mochila and the floor it stands on (`chao`, in cells of half a metre). Sold by a kind of shop, `moveis` (`shops.json`), which a bairro has by standing a stall (`kit_baixa.banca_de_moveis`; Zango's is the Móveis do Kota Beto, by the road). **Prices are a first guess and the founder's to set**: a chair 1 500 Mk, so that the least of families can buy one on its first day; a sofa 45 000.
- **Carried home.** A thing bought is in the Mochila, as bread is: a chair is a third of it, a sofa all of it, so one is carried at a time and there are many journeys in a furnished house. Nothing is delivered yet (a kupapata's work, 6.2).
- **Set down where one stands.** In their own house a player opens "Móveis": each thing they carry is offered ("Pôr aqui: Cadeira de plástico") and is set down a pace before them, its front to them; the thing before them can be turned a quarter ("Rodar") or taken up again ("Guardar na Mochila"). One thumb, no dragging, nothing to aim. Moving a thing is taking it up and setting it down.
- **It cannot be wrong.** A house's floor is a plan of half-metre cells (D-5.8), and a thing stands on whole cells of it: only floor of the inside, never a wall, the doorway, the step before the door or another thing, and never where somebody stands (`mayStand` in `packages/world/src/casas.ts`). The phone asks the rule first, so as not to offer what will be refused; **the zone asks it again and decides**, takes the thing out of the Mochila and writes where it stands in one transaction (`home_things`, migration 0019), and says what is set down, whole, to the house's player. A thing is in the Mochila or on the floor, never both and never neither.
- **It is in the way.** Where a thing stands its own family does not walk: its cells are laid into the house's ground at the zone and on the phone, by the same rule as the walls.
- **It stays.** What is set down is said as the player comes in, and is where it was left.
- **Each thing is a model of its own** in the pack of homes (3 to 4 KB each, drawn in `art/scripts/kit_casas.py`), fetched the first time one of its kind is seen. The pack's build refuses a thing of the catalogue with no model, a model with no thing, and a model wider than its floor.
- **Measured.** The first download: 299,6 KB of 300. Being inside, with the house, its doors and its things: 6,6 KB, fetched once. The pack of homes: 25 KB with all six things.

## What "done" means for this phase

From the roadmap: a player has a home that looks like them, friends visit, rent is paid or the home is lost and nothing in it is; the family's house is where they were born. Measured: an interior's weight and its first view on the slow profile, and a full home's frame time on the reference phone.
