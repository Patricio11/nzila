## 3. Where we can honestly be better

1. **The front door.** No email, under a minute, the megabytes stated before the tap.
2. **One economy with consequences.** Rent, wages, supply contracts, kixikila and insurance between players, all through a ledger that balances to zero.
3. **Belonging.** Respeito by bairro, turmas, table games, the culto, weddings, the Carnaval.
4. **The data promise.** A meter, a saving mode, a size on every download, and a zero-rating deal if we can sign one.
5. **Craft.** One light and one art bible.

## 4. Where they are ahead today, plainly

- They exist, have players and ship updates. We have documents.
- They have more careers than we plan for beta.
- They have voice.
- They have a recognisable brand in the feed.
